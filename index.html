Purpose

Lotto Simulator is a mathematical, statistical, research and entertainment simulator. The application explores different combinations of numbers using mathematics, statistics, probabilistic methods, historical data analysis, mathematical models, algorithmic generation, simulations and comparative analysis.

Model results reflect mathematical and statistical modeling of possible combinations and are not a promise of future results.

Each official lottery drawing is an independent random event. Historical results, mathematical models, frequencies, statistics, simulations, Supreme Judge and other Lotto Simulator tools do not guarantee future winnings.

Frequently asked questions
What is Lotto Simulator?

Lotto Simulator is a mathematical and statistical lottery simulator: a combination generator, draw results and archive, number statistics, a checker for your own lines and a 3D draw machine. It is made for exploration, learning and entertainment.

Does Lotto Simulator sell tickets or accept bets?

No. Lotto Simulator is not a lottery operator: it does not sell tickets, accept bets or money to enter draws, or pay out winnings. Real tickets are sold only by official operators.

Do the models guarantee a win?

No. Every official draw is an independent random event. Models, statistics and simulations help you explore combinations, but they do not predict future results or increase your chance of winning.

Where do the draw results come from?

From the publicly available official results of the lottery operators. The app updates them automatically, and you can check the source and update date in the “Official results” section. The result published by the operator is always the valid one.

What is the difference between FREE and PRO?

FREE includes the simulation, line generation, draw results and basic statistics. PRO unlocks advanced mathematical models and analysis tools; some of them can be tried for free a limited number of times. You can cancel the subscription at any time in subscription management.

What data does the app use?

Saved combinations and settings are stored on your device. No registration is needed for the free features; e-mail sign-in is required only for advanced features and PRO. Optional analytics is turned on only with your consent. See the Privacy Policy for details.
