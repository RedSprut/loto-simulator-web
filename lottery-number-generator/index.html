Frequently asked questions

Does a number generator improve my chances?

No. Every combination has the same probability, whether you choose it yourself or a generator does. A generator saves time and avoids bias; it cannot predict results.

Are random numbers better than birthdays or patterns?

They are not more likely to win. However, many players choose dates and patterns, so a random line is less likely to share a prize with other winners.

How does this generator work?

It picks the required number of distinct numbers from each pool with crypto.getRandomValues, entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
