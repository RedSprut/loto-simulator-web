Frequently asked questions

How do you play EuroMillions?

Choose 5 main numbers from 1 to 50. You also choose 2 Lucky Stars from 1 to 12. Draw days: Tuesday and Friday.

What are the odds of winning the EuroMillions jackpot?

Under the current rules, the chance of winning the jackpot with one line is 1 in 139,838,160.

Are hot or overdue EuroMillions numbers more likely to be drawn?

No. Every draw is independent and each number has the same chance in every draw. Hot, cold and overdue numbers describe past draws; they do not predict future ones.

Where do the EuroMillions results come from?

From the official results published by Lottery.ie. Lotto Simulator checks for new results automatically after every draw. Always confirm prizes with the official operator.

Does Lotto Simulator sell EuroMillions tickets?

No. Lotto Simulator is an independent simulation and statistics tool. It does not sell tickets, accept bets or pay prizes, and it is not affiliated with any lottery operator.
