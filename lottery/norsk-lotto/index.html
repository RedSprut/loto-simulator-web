Frequently asked questions

How do you play Norsk Lotto?

Choose 7 main numbers from 1 to 34. Each draw also includes one supplementary number, drawn from the remaining numbers; it only counts for certain secondary prize tiers. Draw days: Saturday.

What are the odds of winning the Norsk Lotto jackpot?

Under the current rules, the chance of winning the jackpot with one line is 1 in 5,379,616.

Are hot or overdue Norsk Lotto numbers more likely to be drawn?

No. Every draw is independent and each number has the same chance in every draw. Hot, cold and overdue numbers describe past draws; they do not predict future ones.

Where do the Norsk Lotto results come from?

From the official results published by Norsk Tipping. Lotto Simulator checks for new results automatically after every draw. Always confirm prizes with the official operator.

Does Lotto Simulator sell Norsk Lotto tickets?

No. Lotto Simulator is an independent simulation and statistics tool. It does not sell tickets, accept bets or pay prizes, and it is not affiliated with any lottery operator.
