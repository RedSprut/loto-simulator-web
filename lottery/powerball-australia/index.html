Frequently asked questions

How do you play Powerball Australia?

Choose 7 main numbers from 1 to 35. You also choose 1 Powerball number from 1 to 20. Draw days: Thursday.

What are the odds of winning the Powerball Australia jackpot?

Under the current rules, the chance of winning the jackpot with one line is 1 in 134,490,400.

Are hot or overdue Powerball Australia numbers more likely to be drawn?

No. Every draw is independent and each number has the same chance in every draw. Hot, cold and overdue numbers describe past draws; they do not predict future ones.

Where do the Powerball Australia results come from?

From the official results published by The Lott. Lotto Simulator checks for new results automatically after every draw. Always confirm prizes with the official operator.

Does Lotto Simulator sell Powerball Australia tickets?

No. Lotto Simulator is an independent simulation and statistics tool. It does not sell tickets, accept bets or pay prizes, and it is not affiliated with any lottery operator.
