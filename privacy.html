1. Who processes the data

Lotto Simulator processes data necessary for the operation of the app, account, and subscription. The current contact information for the operator and support team is provided via the "Support" link.

2. What data is processed

  • Supabase UUID account ID, email, confirmation status, and technical login information.
  • User-saved combinations, settings, language, and data explicitly submitted by the user to app functions.
  • App version, platform and limited technical request data, including the IP address when the server is contacted, for operation, security and abuse prevention. Without consent, only an anonymous visit counter is kept for statistics: country of the visit (derived by the server from the request address), platform, your analytics choice, network type (for example home provider, mobile operator, data centre or VPN), a technical assessment of the request as ordinary or automated (from the browser type) and whether an account was signed in — without the account itself. Neither the address nor any device, session or account identifier enters the counter; a truncated hash of the address serves only as a temporary rate-limiting key and is deleted after 7 days.
  • Together with the visit counter, browser-independent technical characteristics of the device are transmitted: screen size, number of touch points, platform, time zone, the set of system fonts (a bit mask over a fixed list, without font names), the number of processor cores, the audio sample rate, the graphics processor family and the maximum drawing area size.
  • These characteristics serve a single purpose — recognising the devices that belong to the app owner, so that his own visits do not reach the service notifications. The server converts them into a pseudonym using its own secret key, compares the result with the list of the owner's devices and stores nothing at all for anybody else. They are not stored in the visit counter and are not used to count or divide visitors.
  • The iPhone, iPad and Android apps do not read these technical characteristics and do not use device recognition. On iPhone and iPad, an installation is identified by a random identifier created by the app and kept in the device keychain; it is used only to protect free trial runs and to deliver push notifications.
  • Only with analytics consent: a random identifier of the browser or app installation, sessions, opened sections and features used, active time, traffic source (referring site domain and campaign tags), device type, browser, operating system, language, time zone, screen size and approximate location (country, region, city) derived on the server from the IP address. The IP address is not stored: a pseudonymous network identifier is kept instead, from which the IP address cannot be recovered.
  • Only with separate device recognition consent: technical browser characteristics that allow the same device to be recognised after storage is cleared.
  • Subscription status, product, merchant, expiration date, and transaction IDs received from RevenueCat, Apple, Google Play, or Paddle.

Lotto Simulator does not receive or store your full card number, CVV, bank password, or store payment details.

3. What the data is used for

  • To log in, sync your account, and maintain unified access to PRO across the user's devices.
  • To provide Free and PRO features, restore purchases, and verify current access rights.
  • For security, abuse prevention, diagnostics, support, and compliance with legal obligations.
  • Notifications are available only with user permission; permission can be revoked in device or app settings.
  • For statistics and improving the app, only with consent: how many people use the app, where visitors come from, which countries they are in and which features are used. This data is not used for advertising, is not sold and is not shared with third parties for their own purposes.

4. Legal grounds

Data is processed to fulfill the contract, comply with the law, protect legitimate interests in the security and reliability of the service, and on the basis of consent where required, such as for notifications.

Analytics and device recognition are based solely on consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Information is stored on or read from the device for these purposes only after consent, in accordance with section 3-15 of the Norwegian Electronic Communications Act (ekomloven) and the ePrivacy rules. Counting visits per country is based on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). It writes nothing to the device; in a browser it reads the technical device characteristics listed in section 2 solely to leave out the app owner’s own visits, and the server keeps them only for the owner’s devices. The iPhone, iPad and Android apps do not read these characteristics.

5. Analytics, Device Storage and Your Choice

Until you make a choice, the app stores no analytics identifiers and sends no usage statistics. Regardless of your choice, only an anonymous visit counter is kept: country, platform, network type, an automated-request flag and whether an account was signed in — without identifiers, cookies or entries on the device (the details are in section 2). On your first visit a notice with equally prominent “Decline”, “Customize” and “Accept” buttons is shown. You can change your choice at any time in “Privacy settings” at the bottom of this page or in the “About the application” window.

  • Necessary storage (always): language, theme, sign-in session, your consent choice and the data of features you use.
  • Analytics (only with consent): localStorage entries loto_an_profile_v2 (random identifier), loto_an_session_v2 (session, ends after 30 minutes of inactivity), loto_an_queue_v2 (events not yet sent), loto_an_first_touch_v2 (first traffic source), loto_an_user_v2 (account sign-in marker used to count sign-ins and sign-outs) and loto_an_legacy_v2 (identifier from the previous app version, kept only to delete its data at your request). No cookies are used for analytics.
  • Device recognition (separate consent, only together with analytics): a technical browser fingerprint used to recognise the same device.
  • Google Analytics 4 (only with analytics consent): after you press “Accept”, the Google Analytics tag of Google LLC (for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: Google Ireland Limited) is loaded as an independent control counter of visits and product events: page view, combination generation, opening a PRO feature, the PRO screen, checkout start and a confirmed purchase (without amounts). No user identifier is sent, Google Signals and ad personalisation are off, IP anonymisation is requested; Google sets the cookies _ga and _ga_* (kept for up to 2 years). With “Decline” the tag is not loaded and nothing is sent to Google; withdrawing consent disables the tag and deletes these cookies on this site. Google processes the data under its own privacy policy.

Withdrawing consent immediately stops collection, removes these entries from the device and requests deletion of this device’s analytics data on the server. Declining also deletes data collected on this device by the previous app version. Automated browsers, test and local environments are not counted in statistics.

6. Service Providers

This service may use Supabase for account and server data, RevenueCat for subscription status, Apple and Google Play for in-app purchases, Paddle for web payments, as well as hosting, email delivery, and notification providers. Each provider processes data according to its own terms and applicable law.

Our own statistics are processed in Supabase and are not shared with advertising services. With your analytics consent, independent statistics are kept in parallel in Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited), see section 5; without consent nothing is sent to Google services. Approximate location is determined on our servers; IP addresses are not passed to third-party geolocation services.

7. International Data Transfer

Suppliers may process data in other countries. Where necessary, legally required transfer mechanisms and contractual safeguards are used.

8. Retention Period

Data is retained only for as long as necessary for the account, app functionality, security, accounting, and legal responsibilities. Subscription and transaction records may be retained longer if required by law or to protect the rights of the user and operator.

Analytics events are kept for no longer than 13 months and then deleted; anonymous counters of visits per country are kept for up to 25 months. The log of consent decisions (random choice identifier, decision, policy version, date, platform, language and country, without IP address or account) is kept for up to 3 years as proof of consent.

9. User Rights

Depending on the country, the user may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability, objection to processing, or withdrawal of consent. Some records cannot be deleted immediately if legally required to be retained.

You can delete this device’s analytics data at any time with the “Delete this device’s analytics data” button in “Privacy settings”.

10. Safety and Children

Technical and organizational security measures are used, including access control and server-side subscription verification. No system guarantees absolute security. The Service is not intended for children under the age of digital consent established by applicable law.

11. Changes and contact

For significant changes, the document date will be updated and, where necessary, a notification will be displayed. For privacy questions, please use the official support channel.
